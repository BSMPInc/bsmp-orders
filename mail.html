Mail settings

Managers only — the connection between this app and the Gmail mailboxes.

Mail proxy

The mail proxy is a small Cloudflare Worker that holds the Google credentials and talks to Gmail — this app never sees the keys. Paste its address here once it's deployed.

Mailboxes

Each address shows as a tag on the mail cards (INFO / SALES) and as a "Reply as" choice in the composer.

Signatures

Added automatically to the bottom of every email sent from that address — replies and new emails both. Leave one blank to send without a signature. Add a logo and it's embedded inside the email under your signature text (never a blocked "load images" placeholder) at the width you set with the slider. Shared by the whole team, and separate from the signature set inside Gmail itself (Gmail's only applies to mail written at gmail.com).

Keyboard shortcuts

Ctrl+R — reply to the open conversation  ·  Ctrl+M — new email  ·  Ctrl+Shift+A — attach files (reply or new email, whichever is open)  ·  Ctrl+Enter — send what you're typing  ·  Esc — close a popup. Ctrl+C and Ctrl+A still copy and select-all as normal; Ctrl+R goes back to being browser refresh when no conversation is open.
