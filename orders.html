Job Numbering

Format is a letter + two digits, with a line suffix (e.g. U22-1). New POs get the next base number; each line item in a PO increments the suffix. After 99 the letter rolls forward (U99 → V00).

Quick start — how this app works

Adding orders
Use Add Order to enter a job by hand, or AI Extract to drop in a customer PO (PDF or photo) and let it fill the fields for you. Each line item gets an internal job number automatically.
Open Orders
The left list is every active job; click one to see its details on the right. The toolbar lets you search, filter by status or process, hide invoiced jobs, and show only orders with Missing info (the amber ⚠ badge flags a job that's missing a due date, qty, price, drawing, or PO #).
Tracking progress
On a job's detail, the Current Process icons show where it stands — they turn green as steps are checked off. Internal steps are checked off on the Work Queues page (the shop-floor task lists). The process icons update automatically as work gets done.
Outside processes & POs
Steps like plating or material purchasing are tracked on Need PO (assign the vendor & our PO #) and Incoming / Outgoing (what's out and what's due back). Switch Need PO to Group for PO to pool everything going to one vendor onto a single purchase order. Every assigned PO is logged under Issued POs.
Tube Cut List
Under Tools — plan tube/bar cuts for least waste. Drop in a drawing for AI to read the lengths, or type them in, then print a cut sheet for the saw.
Getting around
The ☰ menu button (top-left) shows or hides the side menu — handy on a tablet. On a computer you can also just move the mouse to the left edge to peek at it.
Tip: most things on a job — status, dates, prices — can be clicked and edited right in place.

While it’s on hold the task is hidden from the Today board. Leave the date blank to hold it until someone releases it — with a date it comes back on its own that morning. The job keeps its due date, schedule and Work Queue spot either way.
